## The operator journey

1. **Watch.** Chronicle exposes a [health endpoint](/chronicle/hosting/configuration/health-endpoint/) and can [alert on observer failures](/chronicle/hosting/alerting-on-observer-failures/). Start from an alert rather than from a user report.
2. **Find the stuck observer.** [Diagnostics](/chronicle/operations/diagnostics/) lists what to look at, in order.
3. **Read the failure.** The failed partition carries the error message and the sequence number it stopped at.
4. **Choose a recovery.** [Recovering from failures](/chronicle/operations/recovering-from-failures/) explains retry, partition replay, full observer replay and clearing quarantine, and when each one fits.
5. **Confirm.** [Recover a failed partition](/chronicle/operations/recover-a-failed-partition/) ends with what done looks like.

Chronicle already retries a failed partition on its own, with a backoff. You step in when the retries are used up, or when you know retrying cannot help.
